    Operators Manual limits by engine¶

    These are the limits the app checks every flight against, taken from each engine's Rotax Operators Manual. The same table, for your workspace's engine, is under Settings → Engine in the app. How a limit becomes an event — minimum durations, phase suppression, the 30 s merge gap — is explained in Operators Manual limits and events.

    Sources¶

    Engine Operators Manual Rated power Turbocharged
    916iS OM-916 i/C24, Edition 0 / Rev. 1, December 2023 157 hp / 117 kW at 5,800 rpm yes
    915iS OM-915 i A / C24, Edition 0 / Rev. 4, September 2022 (Ref. No. OM-915 i A / C24, Part No. 898851) 139 hp / 104 kW at 5,800 rpm yes
    912iS OM-912 i Series, Edition 2 / Rev. 2, September 01 2025 100 hp / 73.5 kW at 5,800 rpm no

    When each limit is checked¶

    Phase suppression and minimum durations for the 916iS profile. Other engines use the same rules unless noted in the app.

    Limit Checked
    Idle RPM minimum must persist 30 s; engine at or above 1,000 rpm; after first reached this flight
    Takeoff RPM (5-min limit) —
    Oil pressure min (>3500 rpm) checked only during climb, cruise, descent; never filterable
    Oil pressure max (>3500 rpm) —
    Oil pressure max (cold start) —
    Oil temp minimum (takeoff) checked only during takeoff, climb, cruise
    Oil temp maximum —
    Oil temp optimal band (low) noted on the card, not an event
    Coolant temp maximum —
    EGT1 maximum —
    EGT2 maximum —
    EGT3 maximum —
    EGT4 maximum —
    Manifold pressure maximum —
    Fuel pressure minimum must persist 10 s; engine at or above 1,000 rpm
    Fuel pressure maximum must persist climb 10 s, cruise 30 s, descent 10 s; not checked during taxi, takeoff, landing
    Bus voltage minimum engine at or above 1,000 rpm
    Bus voltage maximum —
    Oil pressure min (<3500 rpm) must persist 5 s; engine at or above 1,000 rpm; never filterable

    Bus voltage limits are not Operators Manual limits; they are a check for 12 V installations that the app includes because a charging fault shows there first.
